2. RESERVATION VALIDATION & CONFLICT RESOLUTION WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  [User Input] ---> Submit Booking Request (User ID, Resource ID, Start, End, Purpose)
                           |
                           v
              +--------------------------+
              | 1. User Existence Check  | --(Invalid)--> [Reject: User Not Found]
              +--------------------------+
                           | (Valid)
                           v
              +--------------------------+
              | 2. Role Auth Check       | --(Unauthorized)--> [Reject: Role Denied]
              +--------------------------+
                           | (Authorized)
                           v
              +--------------------------+
              | 3. Duration Cap Check    | --(Exceeds Cap)--> [Reject: Duration Exceeded]
              +--------------------------+
                           | (Within Limit)
                           v
              +--------------------------+
              | 4. Operating Hours Check | --(Outside Hours)--> [Reject: Outside 08:00-20:00]
              +--------------------------+
                           | (Valid Hours)
                           v
              +--------------------------+
              | 5. Interval Overlap Test |
              | max(StartA, StartB) <    | --(Overlap Found)--> [Reject: Schedule Conflict]
              | min(EndA, EndB)          |
              +--------------------------+
                           | (No Conflict)
                           v
              +--------------------------+
              | Generate Booking ID      |
              | Status = CONFIRMED       |
              | Persist to JSON          |
              +--------------------------+
                           |
                           v
               [Return Booking Confirmation]
